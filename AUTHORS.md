# Authors

## TIMES.jl (Julia/JuMP translation)

- James Glynn, ESMA Ltd (Ireland) — project lead

TIMES.jl builds on the TIMES2JuMP feasibility study for IEA-ETSAP
(James Glynn and Siddharth Krishna, 2026) and on the earlier
[TIMES.jl prototype](https://github.com/olejandro/times.jl) by
Olexandr Balyk, with review contributions from Oscar Dowson.

## The TIMES GAMS code this is translated from

The code owner of TIMES is the IEA Energy Technology Systems Analysis
Programme (ETSAP). The original authors and contributors are listed in
[AUTHORS.md of the TIMES_model repository](https://github.com/etsap-TIMES/TIMES_model/blob/master/AUTHORS.md).
Current TIMES maintainer: Antti Lehtilä ([@Antti-L](https://github.com/Antti-L)).

Original authors of ETSAP TIMES (by contracting party): Ken Noble (Australia),
Denise van Regemorter (Belgium), Amit Kanudia (Canada), Richard Loulou
(Canada), Uwe Remme (Germany), Peter Schaumann (Germany), Günter Schmid
(Germany), GianCarlo Tosato (Italy), Tom Kram (Netherlands), Tomas Larsson
(Sweden), Gary Goldstein (USA).
